Vanity Resurfacing

Bathroom and kitchen vanities take a beating from daily use, water exposure, and cleaning products. Our vanity resurfacing service repairs chips, sands out imperfections, and applies a durable new coating that restores the original shine without removing and replacing the cabinet or countertop. This is one of the fastest ways to update a dated bathroom or kitchen without a full renovation budget.

We work with laminate, cultured marble, porcelain, and fiberglass vanity tops, matching color and finish so the results blend seamlessly with the rest of your space. Vanity resurfacing is often completed in a single visit, with minimal downtime for your household or business.

Jacuzzi Reglazing

Whirlpool and jacuzzi tubs are harder to keep looking new because of their jets, contours, and constant water exposure. Our jacuzzi reglazing service strips away stains, mineral buildup, and worn finish, then repairs any surface damage before applying a fresh, non-slip coating that is built to handle regular use.

Because jacuzzi tubs have more detailed surfaces than a standard bathtub, our technicians take extra care around jets and fittings to protect functionality while restoring appearance. The result is a smoother, more sanitary tub that looks and feels brand new.

Tile Refinishing and Reglazing

Discolored grout, cracked tile, and dated color schemes make a bathroom or kitchen feel worn no matter how clean it is. Our tile refinishing and top tile reglazing service cleans, repairs, and recoats wall and floor tile, giving you a fresh, uniform finish without removing a single tile.

This service works well for showers, tub surrounds, and kitchen backsplashes, and it can be paired with grout repair for a complete refresh. Tile refinishing is a fraction of the cost and time of a full tile replacement, with results that hold up to regular cleaning.

Tub Chip Repair

A single chip in a bathtub can expose the underlying material to water damage and rust if left untreated. Our tub chip repair service fills, sands, and blends damaged areas so the repaired spot is smooth and nearly invisible, then seals it with a matching coating.

Chip repair can be performed as a standalone fix or combined with a full reglazing for tubs with more extensive wear. Addressing chips early helps extend the life of your bathtub and prevents small damage from becoming a bigger problem.

Fiberglass Repair

Fiberglass tubs and shower pans can develop cracks, soft spots, or flexing over time, especially in older units. Our fiberglass repair service reinforces and seals damaged areas before recoating, restoring structural integrity along with appearance.

Skipping repair and coating over damaged fiberglass leads to premature failure, so our technicians inspect the surface carefully and address any underlying issues first. The result is a stable, watertight surface ready for years of use.

Grout Repair

Cracked or missing grout lets water seep behind tile, leading to mold, mildew, and more costly repairs down the line. Our grout repair service removes damaged grout, repacks the joints, and seals them to keep water where it belongs.

Grout repair pairs well with tile refinishing for a complete restoration, or can be done on its own as routine maintenance. Fresh, sealed grout also makes ongoing cleaning easier and helps your tile look sharper for longer.
